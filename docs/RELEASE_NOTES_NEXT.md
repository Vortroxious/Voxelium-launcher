# Voxelium for iOS: next release (draft)

## New look
- Voxelium now has the same interface as Voxelium for Android, made for iPhone in portrait. iPad works in every orientation, and the game still plays in landscape.
- **Tabs:** Play, Install, Accounts, Controls and Settings. JIT and Diagnostics are now inside Settings.
- **Play:** your last played version on a big card with Play, and your other versions below it.
- **Install:** search Minecraft's versions, pick a mod loader and build in one sheet, and open modpacks from the top of the page.
- **Version page:** tap "…" on a version to see its mods, resource packs, shader packs, worlds and settings.
- **Controls:** edit your layout in portrait on a small preview, or use the full-screen editor. You can now import and share layout files.
- **Logs:** a new page with filters and Share. If Minecraft stops with an error, Voxelium shows why and lets you share the logs.
